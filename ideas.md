# RenoEstimate SG — Design Direction

## Three Possible Approaches

### The Quiet Renovation Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm editorial planning tool that turns renovation uncertainty into a calm, clearly structured estimate. It feels grounded, local, and practical rather than sales-driven.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Tropical Workshop
**Very Brief Intro:** A friendly, tactile visual language inspired by material swatches, timber samples, and a Singapore home in soft daylight. It is more expressive and illustrated, while remaining usable.
**Probability:** 0.04

### Blueprint Without the Blue
**Very Brief Intro:** A crisp architectural interface based on annotated plans, fine rules, and understated measurement cues. It emphasizes confidence and clarity with a technical but approachable tone.
**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Approach: The Quiet Renovation Ledger

### Design Movement
Warm modern editorial utility: a calm financial-planning interface with the restraint of contemporary Singaporean residential design.

### Core Principles
1. Make every choice legible and low-pressure through clear labels, short explanatory copy, and generous breathing room.
2. Keep the calculator visibly practical: structured inputs, exact currency formatting, and a transparent cost breakdown.
3. Use material-inspired warmth sparingly so information, not decoration, remains the focal point.
4. Prefer considered asymmetry and horizontal editorial rhythm over a generic centered landing-page stack.

### Color Philosophy
The site uses an off-white, paper-like ground to lower visual noise; deep charcoal carries the serious planning information; terracotta is the ownable action color, evoking fired clay and newly finished homes. Soft sage green is used for reassurance and estimate feedback, not as a competing brand color.

### Layout Paradigm
The homepage is an editorial split composition: the estimator occupies a substantial left work surface while the result panel is presented as a right-side budget ledger. Supporting content unfolds as full-width bands with restrained rules, rather than repeated floating card grids.

### Signature Elements
1. A simple roofline-and-measure mark that reads as a home plan rather than a property logo.
2. Hairline rules and compact all-caps section labels that resemble a printed construction estimate.
3. Terracotta estimate accents paired with a small sage status dot to suggest a calm, useful planning tool.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are direct and reassuring. Form controls have obvious states, focus treatment is high-contrast, and calculations reveal the estimate without a modal or detour. The user should always feel in control of the assumptions.

### Animation
Use only subtle opacity and transform transitions under 220ms. The result area softly fades and rises into place after calculation. Buttons use a brief scale-down press response. All non-essential animation is disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

### Typography System
Use **Fraunces** for primary editorial headlines to add domestic warmth and **DM Sans** for dense, highly readable interfaces. Headlines have deliberate character and tighter line lengths; labels use small tracked uppercase DM Sans; amounts use tabular numerals for scanning.

### Brand Essence
**RenoEstimate SG turns early renovation choices into a clear Singapore home budget, before a homeowner starts collecting quotes.**

Calm, practical, and transparent.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and gently confident; CTAs name the practical benefit; microcopy distinguishes guidance from a guarantee.

Example headline: “Get a clearer starting budget for your flat.”

Example CTA: “Calculate my renovation range”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact terracotta roofline meeting a vertical measurement tick, designed as a simple solid symbol that can sit beside the wordmark or work alone as a favicon. The wordmark uses the display typeface with an understated sans-serif descriptor.

### Signature Brand Color
**Terracotta Estimate — #B95E40**

## Style Decisions

- No internal setup notes, implementation references, or placeholder publishing instructions appear in the interface. Every visible line must read as calm homeowner-facing guidance.
- Terracotta Estimate `#B95E40` is reserved for the primary calculation action, estimate accents, and brand marks. Secondary commercial or download actions are visually quieter.
- Photography is paired with planning cues—measurement, material labels, estimate annotations, or ledger framing—so it reinforces utility rather than becoming generic renovation lifestyle decoration.
